The problem in one paragraph

A traveller has limited hours and money but many places to see. Each place has an enjoyment value, a ticket price, a visit time and a position on a road network. The system chooses the best set of places that fits both limits, then finds the shortest route between them.

Sample data in this site

12 attractions and 12 locations with 19 road links. One attraction, Valparai Viewpoint, has no road connection on purpose, to show how the system reports an unreachable place.

Design and Analysis of Algorithms mini project · KIT – Kalaignarkarunanidhi Institute of Technology · BE Computer Science and Engineering. Attraction data is sample data for demonstration.
